01 · Problem

Clients pay suppliers in Embat. Payroll? Not yet.

Finance teams at multi-entity clients already run supplier payments through Embat, but payroll — the largest batch of people payments — still sits outside, in bank portals or payroll software. That forces duplicate work bank by bank and entity by entity, breaks the consolidated payments view and leaves payroll without Embat's traceability and controls.

Users

Finance team paying suppliers in Embat; payroll/HR prepares payroll. Different approvers.

Context

Multi-entity and multi-bank: 6 companies/4 banks/400 employees; a UK group of 20 companies.

Friction

Payroll in each bank portal, entity by entity; no consolidated view and no Embat audit trail.

Cause (confirmed): payroll data lives in HR software (e.g. Factorial), not in the ERP; the payments pipeline exists, but the source and its requirements (confidentiality, approvers, deadline) sit outside.

02 · Strategic fit

The strategy is simple: more payments through Embat.

Strategy

Increase the number of payments running through the platform. Payroll is the big missing batch.

Demand

Several clients have already asked for it; it is not an isolated request.

Existing leverage

Bank connectivity, configurable approval engine, reconciliation and employee payments via AP.

Cost of inaction

Volume and stickiness stay outside; the client still needs another tool.

Corporate Payments in Embat — the pipeline exists; the payroll source is missing.

03 · The Bet

Capture volume, don't invent a module.

If payroll becomes another flow inside Corporate Payments, the client runs its entire payment cycle in Embat and gains one more reason to stay.

06 · Risks

What can go wrong (and what we do).

Risk

Confidentiality

Salary is sensitive personal data; unauthorised access breaks trust.

→ RBAC by role/entity, audit trail and Security review before the pilot.

Risk

Payroll deadline

Failing leaves employees unpaid; the cost is higher than with suppliers.

→ Cut-off alerts, clear statuses and testing with the pilot.

Risk

Heterogeneous Excel

Different formats per provider and IBAN/entity mapping errors.

→ Standard template, validation and preview; never a silent partial send.

Reconciliation: banks aggregate; v1 at batch level. Scope creep: calculation and filings belong to the payroll provider, not Embat.
